About Us

Faith Hope Love Charity Organization is a faith-based community initiative founded on May 10, 2023 by Mr. Gideon Lwamba in Lusaka, Zambia.

While we do not operate out of a single fixed physical location, we actively move across local communities to bring aid, hope, and practical assistance directly to those who need it most. Our foundation rests on the truth that we are saved to serve.

"Three things will last forever—faith, hope, and love—and the greatest of these is love." — 1 Corinthians 13:13

"Those who are gracious to the poor lend to the Lord, and the Lord will fully repay them." — Proverbs 19:17

Our Vision

A transformed, hopeful community where no individual is left without food, shelter, or care, and where every person is empowered to discover and fulfill their God-given potential through faith, hope, and love.

Our Mission

To reflect God's love by providing essential physical relief, educational support, and talent development to those in need—empowering individuals, restoring dignity, and serving our community with compassion and charity in action.

Our Core Pillars

Faith

Assurance and conviction, moving forward with unwavering trust in God’s guidance.

Hope

Confident expectation in God's faithfulness, inspiring a brighter vision for tomorrow.

Love

Actions of kindness, unconditional acceptance, and unity in every interaction.

Charity

Putting our beliefs into motion through active, tangible community service.

What We Do

🤝

Essential Relief Aid

Feeding the hungry, quenching the thirsty, clothing the needy, sheltering the homeless, and ministering to the sick through street and orphanage outreaches.

📚

Educational Support

Providing learning opportunities, school materials, and mentorship to children and youth in local schools to help them secure a brighter future.

🌟

Talent Recognition

Identifying, nurturing, and promoting latent gifts and skills within our community to foster self-reliance, confidence, and purpose.
